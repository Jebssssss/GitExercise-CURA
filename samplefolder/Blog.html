Habagat rains persist in Luzon; fair weather over rest of PH

Introduction

MANILA, Philippines — The southwest monsoon or habagat will continue to bring rains in parts of Luzon while the rest of the country will see improving weather conditions on Wednesday, according to the state weather bureau. In the 5 a.m. weather forecast of the Philippine Atmospheric, Geophysical, and Astronomical Services Administration (Pagasa), weather specialist Chenel Dominguez said rains are forecast over the western section of the country.

“Inaasahan na more on concentration ng pag-ulan ay andito sa western section ng ating bansa, particularly sa may Ilocos Region, western section ng Central Luzon, Metro Manila, Cavite, Laguna, Batangas, Oriental Mindoro, Occidental Mindoro, pati na rin sa may Marinduque,” Dominguez said. (Concentrated rains are expected over the western section of the country, particularly in Ilocos Region, western section of Central Luzon, Metro Manila, Cavite, Laguna, Batanas, Oriental Mindoro, and Marinduque.) The eastern portion of the country will experience improving weather conditions with chances of rains in the afternoon and evening due to localized thunderstorms. Palawan, particularly the northern section of it, will see cloudy skies with scattering rains.

A significant amount of rainfall (50 to 100 millimeters) is forecast over Zambales, Bataan, and Occidental Mindoro for 24 hours. With this, she warned of flooding and landslides.

Meanwhile, Visayas and Mindanao are forecast to have fair and hot weather, especially in the afternoon and evening, with chances of localized thunderstorms in the afternoon and evening. No gale warning is raised in any seaboards of the country.

Temperature forcasts in the following areas:

  • Metro Manila: 25°C to 30°C
  • Laoag: 25°C to 31°C
  • Baguio: 17°C to 21°C
  • Tagaytay: 23°C to 26°C
  • Tuguegarao: 25°C to 33°C
  • Legazpi: 26°C to 33°C
  • Kalayaan Islands: 27°C to 32°C
  • Puerto Princesa: 26°C to 32°C
  • Iloilo: 26°C to 33°C
  • Cebu: 26°C to 33°C
  • Tacloban: 27°C to 34°C

Tropical depression outside the PAR

Dominguez also shared that the tropical depression monitored outside the Philippine area of responsibility will have no direct effect in the country, moving east-northwestward at 30 kilometers per hour (kph). She added that it continued to move far from the country’s landmass.
